# MyTyles LinkedIn Strategy

## 1. Where We Are Now
MyTyles is currently building a more structured and consistent presence on LinkedIn.

At this stage, our focus is not only on increasing follower numbers. We are working towards understanding which content formats, topics, and conversations are most relevant to our audience and building a recognisable content presence over time.

Established brands in the tiles, bathware, and home-interiors space can provide useful benchmarks for content formats, campaigns, and audience engagement. However, their follower counts and engagement should not be treated as direct performance benchmarks for MyTyles, since brand maturity, audience size, posting history, and market presence differ.

Our approach is therefore to:

- build consistency before chasing scale;
- experiment with different content formats and topics;
- identify what generates meaningful engagement;
- learn from competitors without replicating their content;
- track our own growth over time; and
- gradually develop a recognisable MyTyles voice on LinkedIn.
## 2. Why We Are Using LinkedIn
Our goal on LinkedIn is to build a relevant and engaged audience around MyTyles rather than treating follower growth as an isolated number.

We want our content to introduce more people to MyTyles, give them useful reasons to follow the page, and gradually build enough interest for them to take the next step, whether that means visiting the MyTyles website, viewing our tile collections, reading our content, visiting the Experience Centre, or engaging with the brand.

LinkedIn also gives us an opportunity to strengthen MyTyles' visibility within the tile, architecture, interior design, and home-improvement industries. We want industry professionals and businesses to understand who we are, what we offer, what we stand for, and how MyTyles is growing.

### Our LinkedIn Objectives

1. **Grow a Relevant Audience**  
   Build a stronger follower base consisting of people who are genuinely interested in tiles, interiors, architecture, home improvement, and the wider industry.

2. **Build Brand Awareness**  
   Increase familiarity with MyTyles, our products, our approach, our vision, and what differentiates the brand.

3. **Create Interest in MyTyles**  
   Use useful, educational, inspiring, and brand-led content to give people a reason to continue engaging with MyTyles.

4. **Encourage Meaningful Actions**  
   Where relevant, guide interested audiences towards the next logical step, such as visiting the website, reading a blog, viewing a tile collection, or visiting the MyTyles Experience Centre.

5. **Build Industry Visibility**  
   Increase awareness of MyTyles among architects, interior designers, industry professionals, businesses, and others working within the tiles and interiors ecosystem.

6. **Communicate the MyTyles Brand**  
   Use LinkedIn to communicate our products, expertise, customer experiences, company developments, values, vision, and the people behind the brand.

## 3. Who We Want to Reach

MyTyles uses LinkedIn to connect with people who are interested in tiles, architecture, interior design, home improvement, and the wider building-materials industry.

Our content should serve two purposes: help potential customers understand MyTyles and strengthen our visibility among industry professionals.

### Our Primary Audience Groups

| Audience | What They May Be Interested In | Content We Can Share | Desired Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homeowners & Renovators | Tile ideas, design choices, product suitability, and selection guidance | Educational carousels, design blogs, customer stories | Visit the website, view collections, or visit the Experience Centre |
| Architects & Interior Designers | Design applications, materials, finishes, product variety, and project inspiration | Tile School, design-focused content, product showcases, completed projects | Become familiar with MyTyles and consider its collections for projects |
| Builders & Industry Professionals | Product categories, company developments, project applications, and industry knowledge | Brand updates, product information, company milestones | Build awareness and potential professional relationships |
| Tile & Interiors Industry Community | MyTyles' positioning, vision, product range, activities, and developments | Founder-led content, company stories, events, and brand initiatives | Recognise MyTyles and engage with its professional presence |

### How We Will Approach These Audiences

We will not create every post for every audience.

Instead, each post should have a clear primary audience and purpose. Educational content may introduce MyTyles to homeowners, while company updates and design-focused posts may be more relevant to architects, designers, and industry professionals.

The writing style, information depth, and call to action should reflect the audience and the purpose of the post.

### Our Guiding Principle

**We want to attract the right audience, not simply increase follower numbers.**

Follower growth matters, but relevant engagement, website interest, Experience Centre enquiries, and industry recognition are also important indicators of progress.
