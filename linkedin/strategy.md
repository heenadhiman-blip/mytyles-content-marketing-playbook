# MyTyles LinkedIn Strategy

## 1. Where We Are Now
MyTyles is currently building a more structured and consistent presence on LinkedIn.

At this stage, our focus is not only on increasing follower numbers. We are working towards understanding which content formats, topics, and conversations are most relevant to our audience and building a recognisable content presence over time.

Established brands in the tiles, bathware, and home-interiors space can provide useful benchmarks for content formats, campaigns, and audience engagement. However, their follower counts and engagement should not be treated as direct performance benchmarks for MyTyles, since brand maturity, audience size, posting history, and market presence differ.

Our approach is therefore to:

- build consistency before chasing scale;
- experiment with different content formats and topics;
- identify what generates meaningful engagement;
- learn from competitors without replicating their content;
- track our own growth over time; and
- gradually develop a recognisable MyTyles voice on LinkedIn.
## 2. Why We Are Using LinkedIn
Our goal on LinkedIn is to build a relevant and engaged audience around MyTyles rather than treating follower growth as an isolated number.

We want our content to introduce more people to MyTyles, give them useful reasons to follow the page, and gradually build enough interest for them to take the next step, whether that means visiting the MyTyles website, viewing our tile collections, reading our content, visiting the Experience Centre, or engaging with the brand.

LinkedIn also gives us an opportunity to strengthen MyTyles' visibility within the tile, architecture, interior design, and home-improvement industries. We want industry professionals and businesses to understand who we are, what we offer, what we stand for, and how MyTyles is growing.

### Our LinkedIn Objectives

1. **Grow a Relevant Audience**  
   Build a stronger follower base consisting of people who are genuinely interested in tiles, interiors, architecture, home improvement, and the wider industry.

2. **Build Brand Awareness**  
   Increase familiarity with MyTyles, our products, our approach, our vision, and what differentiates the brand.

3. **Create Interest in MyTyles**  
   Use useful, educational, inspiring, and brand-led content to give people a reason to continue engaging with MyTyles.

4. **Encourage Meaningful Actions**  
   Where relevant, guide interested audiences towards the next logical step, such as visiting the website, reading a blog, viewing a tile collection, or visiting the MyTyles Experience Centre.

5. **Build Industry Visibility**  
   Increase awareness of MyTyles among architects, interior designers, industry professionals, businesses, and others working within the tiles and interiors ecosystem.

6. **Communicate the MyTyles Brand**  
   Use LinkedIn to communicate our products, expertise, customer experiences, company developments, values, vision, and the people behind the brand.
## 3. Who We Want to Reach
## 3. Who We Want to Reach

MyTyles uses LinkedIn to connect with people who are interested in tiles, architecture, interior design, home improvement, and the wider building-materials industry.

Our content should serve two purposes: help potential customers understand MyTyles and strengthen our visibility among industry professionals.

### Our Primary Audience Groups

| Audience | What They May Be Interested In | Content We Can Share | Desired Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homeowners & Renovators | Tile ideas, design choices, product suitability, and selection guidance | Educational carousels, design blogs, customer stories | Visit the website, view collections, or visit the Experience Centre |
| Architects & Interior Designers | Design applications, materials, finishes, product variety, and project inspiration | Tile School, design-focused content, product showcases, completed projects | Become familiar with MyTyles and consider its collections for projects |
| Builders & Industry Professionals | Product categories, company developments, project applications, and industry knowledge | Brand updates, product information, company milestones | Build awareness and potential professional relationships |
| Tile & Interiors Industry Community | MyTyles' positioning, vision, product range, activities, and developments | Founder-led content, company stories, events, and brand initiatives | Recognise MyTyles and engage with its professional presence |

### How We Will Approach These Audiences

We will not create every post for every audience.

Instead, each post should have a clear primary audience and purpose. Educational content may introduce MyTyles to homeowners, while company updates and design-focused posts may be more relevant to architects, designers, and industry professionals.

The writing style, information depth, and call to action should reflect the audience and the purpose of the post.

### Our Guiding Principle

**We want to attract the right audience, not simply increase follower numbers.**

Follower growth matters, but relevant engagement, website interest, Experience Centre enquiries, and industry recognition are also important indicators of progress.

## 4. What We Want Our LinkedIn Page to Be Known For
## 4. What We Want Our LinkedIn Page to Be Known For

We want MyTyles' LinkedIn presence to be recognised for useful tile-related information, design inspiration, customer experiences, and meaningful updates about the company.

Our aim is to build a page that people follow not only to see tile collections, but also to understand tile selection, learn about design possibilities, and stay connected with MyTyles.

The content should reflect both sides of the brand: **a tile destination for customers and an active participant in the tiles and interiors industry.**

### Our Content Identity

| What We Want to Be Known For | What It Means | How We Can Demonstrate It |
|---|---|---|
| Tile Knowledge & Education | Helping people understand tiles, their applications, and important selection considerations | Tile School carousels, infographics, practical checklists, and educational posts |
| Design Inspiration | Showing different ways tiles can be considered for residential and commercial spaces | Design ideas, blog snippets, tile combinations, and application-focused visuals |
| Customer Experiences | Showing how customers interact with MyTyles and use its products in their spaces | Customer testimonials, completed-home videos, and Experience Centre stories |
| Industry Presence | Building familiarity with MyTyles among architects, designers, builders, and other industry professionals | Company developments, professional collaborations, events, and project-related content |
| Brand Identity & Values | Communicating the people, thinking, vision, and developments behind MyTyles | Founder-led posts, company milestones, team stories, and brand initiatives |
| Product Awareness | Introducing audiences to the tile categories, designs, finishes, and product options available at MyTyles | Product showcases, collection highlights, category-led posts, and website content |

### How This Will Shape Our Content

Our LinkedIn page should maintain a balance between educational, inspirational, customer-focused, and company-led content.

We do not want every post to feel like a product advertisement. Different posts should serve different purposes depending on the topic, target audience, and stage of the customer journey.

For example:

- An educational carousel can help readers understand what to check before selecting tiles.
- A blog-led post can introduce a design topic and encourage readers to visit the full article.
- A customer testimonial can communicate a real experience with MyTyles.
- A founder-led post can share perspectives, company values, or business developments.
- A product-focused post can introduce a relevant tile collection and direct interested readers to the website.

### Our Communication Principles

**1. Useful Before Promotional**

Content should offer information, inspiration, or a meaningful brand update rather than simply asking people to visit the website.

**2. Clear and Approachable**

Use straightforward language that homeowners and industry professionals can understand. Explain technical terms when needed.

**3. Accurate and Responsible**

Avoid exaggerated product claims, unsupported guarantees, and assumptions about suitability. Product information and technical guidance should be verified before publication.

**4. Visually Consistent**

Follow the approved MyTyles brand guidelines for typography, colours, logo usage, and visual presentation.

**5. Relevant Calls to Action**

The CTA should follow naturally from the content. Depending on the post, it may encourage readers to view a blog, visit a collection, share an opinion, or visit the Experience Centre.

**6. Recognisable Without Being Repetitive**

Maintain a consistent brand voice while varying hooks, storytelling approaches, post formats, and CTAs. Individual posts should not feel like copies of one another.

### Our Guiding Principle

**Every post should give the audience a reason to learn about, remember, engage with, or take an interest in MyTyles.**

A strong LinkedIn presence is built through repeated, useful interactions rather than relying only on individual promotional posts.

## 5. Our LinkedIn Content Approach
## 5. Our LinkedIn Content Approach

MyTyles follows a consistent LinkedIn publishing schedule built around three content pillars: Educational Content, Blog Promotion, and Customer Testimonials.

We publish **three times a week, on Tuesday, Thursday, and Friday**.

Each pillar has a different purpose. Educational posts share useful tile knowledge, blog promotions introduce readers to content published on the MyTyles website, and customer testimonials communicate genuine experiences with the brand.

Together, these content types help us build awareness, encourage website visits, and strengthen familiarity with MyTyles.

### 5.1 Our Three Active Content Pillars

| Content Pillar | Purpose | Content Formats | Intended Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Educational Content | Share practical tile knowledge, design guidance, and information that helps readers understand tile-related topics | Carousels, infographics, checklists, and educational graphics | Encourage learning, engagement, and recognition of MyTyles as an informative brand |
| Blog Promotion | Introduce topics covered in existing MyTyles blogs and encourage audiences to read the complete articles | Blog snippets, single-image posts, and blog-based carousels | Generate interest in blog topics and encourage visits to the MyTyles website |
| Customer Testimonials | Share genuine customer experiences and show how people interact with MyTyles | Customer testimonial videos, photographs, and customer-story posts | Build familiarity and trust through authentic customer experiences |

**Current Status: Active**

These are the three content pillars currently used in our LinkedIn publishing approach.

### 5.2 Our Fixed Weekly Publishing Schedule

| Day | Content Pillar | Primary Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Tuesday | Educational Content | Help audiences learn something useful about tiles |
| Thursday | Blog Promotion | Introduce an existing blog and encourage readers to visit the website |
| Friday | Customer Testimonials | Share real customer experiences with MyTyles |

Our publishing days remain consistent to establish a regular content routine.

The topics and creative formats may change from week to week, but the three active content pillars remain the foundation of the schedule.

### 5.3 Educational Content

Educational content is designed to help audiences understand tiles and make more informed decisions.

We can cover topics such as tile materials, finishes, design applications, layout considerations, selection factors, and common misconceptions.

**Examples of educational content:**

- Tile School carousels explaining commonly used tile terms.
- Infographics comparing tile design options.
- Checklists covering factors to consider before choosing tiles.
- Practical explanations of finishes, sizes, patterns, and applications.

Educational content should be easy to understand, visually organised, and technically accurate.

Its primary purpose is to offer value rather than directly promote a product.

### 5.4 Blog Promotion Content

Blog promotion introduces LinkedIn audiences to useful articles already published on the MyTyles website.

The LinkedIn post should give readers enough information to understand the topic and why the article may be relevant to them.

However, it should not reproduce the entire blog. The post should create a natural reason to continue reading.

**Our approach:**

1. Select a relevant, published MyTyles blog.
2. Identify the main idea or takeaway.
3. Develop a concise caption that accurately represents the article.
4. Create a supporting visual or carousel.
5. Include a clear link or direction to the complete blog.

Blog promotions can cover design inspiration, tile selection guidance, comparisons, and other topics already addressed on the website.

**Primary objective:** Encourage interested readers to visit the MyTyles blog.

### 5.5 Customer Testimonials

Customer testimonials allow audiences to hear about real experiences with MyTyles.

These posts may feature customers discussing their visit to the Experience Centre, interactions with the team, tile selection process, or overall experience.

The accompanying LinkedIn copy should support the testimonial rather than repeat everything said in the video.

**Our approach:**

- Introduce the customer experience naturally.
- Highlight a relevant aspect of the story without exaggeration.
- Keep the caption concise and conversational.
- Encourage viewers to watch the testimonial.
- Ensure the content accurately represents the customer's statements.

Customer testimonials should remain authentic and should only be published with the necessary permissions.

**Primary objective:** Build trust and familiarity through real customer experiences.

### 5.7 Choosing Topics and Formats

Every LinkedIn post should have a clear purpose and a relevant audience.

Before developing a post, we should consider:

1. Who is the intended audience?
2. What information or experience are we sharing?
3. Why is this topic relevant to MyTyles?
4. Which format will communicate it most clearly?
5. What action, if any, should we encourage?

A carousel may work well for educational information that needs several slides. A single-image post may be sufficient for a blog promotion, while a video may be more appropriate for a customer testimonial.

The format should be selected according to the content rather than used simply for variety.

### 5.8 Calls to Action Across Our Three Pillars

| Content Pillar | CTA Approach | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Educational Content | Encourage saving, learning, or discussion | "Save this checklist for your next tile selection." |
| Blog Promotion | Encourage readers to visit the full article | "Read the complete article on the MyTyles blog." |
| Customer Testimonials | Encourage viewers to watch the customer's experience | "Watch what our customer had to say about their experience with MyTyles." |

These are illustrative examples. The wording should change according to the post so that our captions do not become repetitive.

A website or Experience Centre CTA can also be included when it is relevant to the content.

### 5.9 How Our Content Supports the Marketing Funnel

Our three content pillars can contribute to different stages of the audience journey.

| Content Pillar | Possible Funnel Contribution | How |
|---|---|---|
| Educational Content | Primarily TOFU, sometimes MOFU | Introduces tile knowledge and helps audiences understand selection factors |
| Blog Promotion | TOFU or MOFU, depending on the article | Encourages readers to learn more about design ideas, options, and comparisons |
| Customer Testimonials | MOFU or BOFU, depending on the message | Helps interested audiences understand genuine customer experiences before taking further action |

These classifications are not fixed. The audience's intent and the specific message determine which funnel stage a post supports.

### 5.10 Maintaining Content Quality

Before publishing, we should check that:

- The information is factually accurate.
- Product claims and technical statements are verified.
- Spelling, grammar, and wording are clear.
- The design follows MyTyles' brand guidelines.
- Captions and visuals communicate the same message.
- Website links lead to the correct destination.
- Customer stories accurately represent the original feedback.
- The CTA is relevant and not unnecessarily promotional.

### 5.11 Reviewing Our Content Approach

We should periodically review the performance of our three active content pillars.

Important indicators include follower growth, impressions, engagement, relevant comments, and website visits where tracking is available.

Rather than focusing only on which post receives the most likes, we should consider what each post was intended to achieve.

For example, an educational carousel may receive saves, while a blog promotion may generate website visits. Both can be valuable, even if their engagement patterns differ.

These observations can help us improve our content while maintaining the established Tuesday, Thursday, and Friday publishing schedule.

### Our Guiding Principle

**Three consistent publishing days. Three active content pillars. A clear purpose behind every post.**

Our current priority is to strengthen Educational Content, Blog Promotion, and Customer Testimonials before gradually introducing additional content categories.
