# MyTyles LinkedIn Strategy

## 1. Where We Are Now
MyTyles is currently building a more structured and consistent presence on LinkedIn.

At this stage, our focus is not only on increasing follower numbers. We are working towards understanding which content formats, topics, and conversations are most relevant to our audience and building a recognisable content presence over time.

Established brands in the tiles, bathware, and home-interiors space can provide useful benchmarks for content formats, campaigns, and audience engagement. However, their follower counts and engagement should not be treated as direct performance benchmarks for MyTyles, since brand maturity, audience size, posting history, and market presence differ.

Our approach is therefore to:

- build consistency before chasing scale;
- experiment with different content formats and topics;
- identify what generates meaningful engagement;
- learn from competitors without replicating their content;
- track our own growth over time; and
- gradually develop a recognisable MyTyles voice on LinkedIn.
