VOLUME 03 · WRITING STANDARDS

Content Guidelines.

One reference for two distinct writing workflows: MyTyles blogs and category page reworks. Use the applicable requirements, verify product claims, and escalate changes that affect SEO strategy.

Blog guidelinesCategory page reworksTechnical safeguardsSubmission checks
Publication review: This volume is consolidated from two supplied internal documents. Confirm public-sharing approval and resolve any source-policy conflicts before deploying to a public GitHub Pages website.

SECTION 01

Start here: Which rules apply?

These two source documents describe different content workflows. Select the appropriate set before drafting.

Blog writing

Maximum 1,350 words in the body; 7 FAQs; meta description up to 150 characters; title tag up to 60 characters; author bio up to 100 words; maximum 3 images including the banner.

Category page reworks

Publishable main body 1,000–1,200 words, excluding FAQs, SEO tables, image recommendations and writer notes; approximately 100–150 words for the introduction; exactly 10 FAQs with answers of 50–60 words each; meta title up to 60 characters and meta description up to 160 characters.

Different rules are intentional

Do not transfer the blog FAQ or metadata limits to category-page reworks. Follow the applicable brief and seek clarification when instructions conflict.

SECTION 02

Shared writing and formatting standards

Apply clarity and consistency while preserving the SEO brief and verified product information.

Voice

Professional, approachable and authoritative without unexplained jargon. Write for homeowners and relevant buyers; category pages should also help architects, designers and contractors compare products.

Structure

Use clear H1, H2 and H3 headings, focused paragraphs, logical transitions and one central idea per paragraph. Remove repetitive filler, generic storytelling and awkward AI-style phrasing.

Language

Use English consistently, including UK spellings such as colour and grey. Write the brand exactly as MyTyles.

Document font

The blog guidelines specify Calibri for category webpage documents: body 11 pt, H1 20 pt, H2 16 pt and H3 14 pt. Align headings and text left, and leave spacing after paragraphs, between long bullets, and after images or tables.

Text emphasis

Bold key terms; use italics for quotes, CTAs or emphasis; use bullet points; highlight keywords in a shade of yellow in working documents.

SECTION 03

Blog writing workflow

The blog-specific rules from New Writing Guidelines For Blogs.

Content and metadata

Body maximum 1,350 words; title tag maximum 60 characters; meta description maximum 150 characters; author bio maximum 100 words.

Seven FAQs

Write 7 question-and-answer FAQs. Use common user questions as H2 or H3 headings; naturally include relevant secondary and long-tail terms, and answer each question concisely and usefully.

SEO and keyword use

Place relevant keywords naturally in headings and copy, add meaningful image alt text, and avoid stuffing. The blog document specifies a 1–2% keyword-density target; do not treat that blog rule as a category-page rule.

Resource links

Add two resource links: one after approximately the first 100 words and one within approximately the final 100 words. Use the supplied anchor and target link naturally in the content body as directed by the SEO instructions. The source describes both placement instructions; confirm precise positioning when the brief is unclear.

Images

Maximum 3 images including the banner. The source requests the MyTyles logo at the top-right of images; confirm that instruction against the current approved brand/visual brief before publishing.

Internal links

Link to relevant blogs, guides and product pages. Keep destinations useful and contextually relevant.

File naming

Use month and year, content-calendar serial number, type (new or repurpose) and blog name. Source example: Oct_2025_Blog#1_New/Repurpose_.

SECTION 04

Category page: writer responsibilities

Humanise the writing without changing the approved SEO strategy.

Writers may change

Sentence structure, natural phrasing, transitions, examples, explanatory wording, paragraph flow, awkward AI wording, tone, readability, minor heading wording, grammar and clarity.

Writers must retain

GSC priority keywords, search intent, approved H1, important H2/H3 topics, pricing and commercial-intent sections, technical caveats, relevant internal links, FAQ rules, URL strategy, category positioning and cannibalisation boundaries.

Category shopper intent

Help shoppers compare application, material, design, finish, colour, size, price and suitability. Avoid blog-like storytelling introductions and unrelated educational filler.

Keywords

Keep primary, secondary and LSI/semantic terms from the SEO brief. Do not remove a GSC-led keyword merely because the phrase sounds awkward; rewrite naturally. Do not chase a fixed keyword density for category pages.

Introduction

Aim for approximately 100–150 words describing the category, possible uses, choices and relevant selection considerations.

SECTION 05

Category page: technical accuracy

No unsupported performance or suitability claims.

Avoid absolutes

Do not claim waterproof, slip-proof, scratch-proof, stain-proof, weather-proof, maintenance-free, never fades, perfect or best without product-specific substantiation.

Slip resistance

Matt does not automatically mean anti-skid. Explain that surface grip may vary and ask readers to check the individual product slip-resistance rating.

Wet areas and terraces

Tiles alone do not form a waterproofing system. Substrate preparation, membrane or treatment, slope, drainage, adhesive, grout, joints and installation all matter.

Porcelain and vitrified absorption

Do not present figures such as below 0.5% as universal. State a numerical absorption claim only if the individual product specification confirms it.

Large-format installation

Where relevant, discuss substrate flatness, adhesive coverage, handling, joint planning, lippage control and experienced installation.

Catalogue verification

Material, colour, size, finish, price, slip property and application must match current MyTyles product information. Never invent specifications.

SECTION 06

Category page: pricing tables

Use verified data and preserve the selling unit.

Price source

Use current supplied MyTyles product or category data. Do not invent price ranges, discounts or starting prices.

Unit consistency

Never mix ₹/sq. ft., ₹/piece, ₹/set and ₹/box in one range. Use a dedicated Unit column or separate tables.

Presentation

Use a meaningful product grouping and a clear price range, with consistent ₹ formatting and units. Treat every price as indicative.

Recommended note

“Prices are indicative and may vary by design, size, finish, quantity, stock and seller updates. Check individual product pages for the latest pricing and availability.”

Avoid sales claims

Do not say lowest price, best price, cheapest or guaranteed price without approved evidence.

SECTION 07

Category page: size tables

Show actual category availability, not generic industry inventories.

Verified sizes only

Use the live category filters, listings or approved source. Do not add a size merely because it is common in the market.

Column order

Tile Size in mm → Tile Size in inches → Tile Size in feet. Example: 600x1200 mm | 24x48 inches | 2x4 feet.

Formatting

Use lowercase x with no spaces, such as 300x600 mm, 12x24 inches and 1x2 feet. Include units in each cell and sort sizes logically.

Conversions

Inches and feet may be reference or nominal values, not exact manufacturing dimensions. Verify actual product dimensions before ordering.

Suitability

Do not claim every size fits every space. Consider cuts, room size, slope, drainage, substrate, handling and installation. Mention the tile calculator where appropriate.

Recommended note

“Sizes shown are reference dimensions based on the available category/product data. Actual product dimensions may vary slightly. Check the individual product page before ordering.”

SECTION 08

Category page: sections and FAQs

Keep each section focused on the correct buying decision.

Material

Discuss tile body, performance and suitability; do not confuse material with colour or finish.

Finish

Discuss reflection, shine, texture, surface feel and cleaning, not material or price.

Colour

Discuss actual shades, matching and appearance, not unsupported performance.

Size

Discuss dimensions, layout, cuts, grout joints, handling and tile calculator use.

Price

Discuss verified ranges, units and indicative pricing only.

FAQ rules

Exactly 10 FAQs for category reworks, each with a 50–60-word answer. This overrides the 7-FAQ blog rule.

SECTION 09

When to ask SEO

Pause before changing strategy or publishing uncertain claims.

Escalate keyword and structure changes

Ask SEO before removing a GSC keyword, deleting or merging a GSC-led heading, or materially changing the H1, approved title tag or meta description.

Escalate catalogue conflicts

Ask when prices, sizes, finishes or technical statements conflict with the live catalogue, or a claim needs qualification.

Escalate links and intent

Ask before changing important anchor targets or if a section may cannibalise another MyTyles category.

SECTION 10

Submission checklists

Use the applicable checklist before submitting content for review.

Blog checklist

Check body length, metadata limits, 7 FAQs, image count, alt text, two resource links, supplied SEO anchors, formatting, internal links and file naming.

Category checklist

Check 1,000–1,200 main-body words, preserved GSC terms and search intent, verified pricing and sizes, separate selling units, technical qualifications, accurate material/finish/colour/size/price sections, exactly 10 FAQs, and no broken sentences.

AI and plagiarism review

The category document requires AI% below 5% and plagiarism below 5%. Treat these as its specified submission checks, not proof of factual accuracy or authorship.

Final accuracy pass

Verify every product-specific statement against approved current catalogue information and resolve unresolved SEO questions before publishing.
